The intensity scale

Four bands that mean the same thing everywhere in the product: high → mid → low → risk. Light reads them as green → orange → yellow → red. Dark swaps the middle two for the Nocturne hues — purple where light uses orange, grey where light uses yellow — so the dark mode never turns into a fruit bowl while still ranking the same way.

The ladder

Same four meanings, two vocabularies. Note the dark ladder still descends: green is brightest, grey is the most recessive — "low" should sink, not shout.

Applied — utilisation, where a scale actually earns its keep

A legend proves nothing. Below, the same fleet data rendered with the bands doing real work: a meter per hub, tiles, and twelve weeks of deployments coloured by band.

One thing I changed on my own. My first pick for dark "low" was #8F98B8, but its luminance (0.318) was almost identical to risk red (0.329) — in greyscale, or to a colourblind operator, the bottom two bands were the same bar. Dark "low" is now #6E7698 (0.185), which also reads better: low should recede.
