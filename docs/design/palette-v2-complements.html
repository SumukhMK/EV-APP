Saffron v2 — amber, paired

Four complementary schemes. The amber stays vivid in every one, because the job it was doing badly — being readable type on white — is now done by the cool partner. Amber is a fill: buttons, bars, the active nav marker. That is the whole point of pairing it.

Backgrounds are pure #FFFFFF. Panels separate with hairlines, not grey fills.
The brown is gone. Links/labels use the complement; where a warm accent is wanted it is rust #C2410C, not mud.
